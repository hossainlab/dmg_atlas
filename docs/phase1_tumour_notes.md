## Label coverage (integrated samples flagged for inclusion, n=59)
- H3 variant (H3.1/H3.2/H3.3) known: 54.2% (32/59) — all Jessa + Collot samples; unknown for DeSisto, Zang, ScPCA and all 22 Okada samples (K27M/K27-altered status known for 100%).
- Assay (cell vs nucleus) known: 100%. Site known: 100% (pons 42, thalamus 17). Age known: 54/59. Tissue timing: autopsy 22 (Okada), resection 5, unknown 32 (Jessa Table S1 'Material' only says 'Tumor').
- Patients with two samples: 4 Jessa patients in both series; PMC-DMG-9 core/edge.

## Deviations and surprises
1. GSE210566 provides 10 multiome RNA samples, not 20 (the other 10 GSMs are ATAC bigwigs).
2. GSE210567 is a pHGG/PFA series: only 17 of 37 samples are midline K27M gliomas.
3. Collot's human deposit is dominated by re-processed Jessa data; only 5 new samples (4 patients). Its Table S1A annotations for the Jessa samples disagree with Jessa's own Table S1 for several IDs — Jessa's table is used.
4. DeSisto per-sample H3 status comes from the ScPCA portal record of the same cohort, not from the paper supplement; only 2 confirmed K27M.
5. LaBelle: paper numbering ≠ GEO numbering; GSE271936 is Xenium, not scRNA.
6. ScPCA 'processed' objects are over-filtered (51 cells for SCPCS000636); use 'filtered'.
7. Okada export carries no integer matrix, but counts are exactly invertible from raw.X and nCount_RNA.
8. Liu: raw-count cell IDs need harmonisation; one sample lacks raw counts.
9. Network grants requested and approved during this track: cells-test.gi.ucsc.edu, static-content.springer.com, scpca-portal-deployer-prod.s3.amazonaws.com, academic.oup.com (the last returned a Cloudflare challenge and was not used).
10. Not fetched: Filbin/Liu paper supplements (per-patient age/variant), DeSisto paper supplement, GSE271936 images/transcripts, Jessa ATAC bigwigs, Collot ST.tar.gz (other track), GSM7846770.

## Audit corrections (2026-10-19; findings DQ-02, DQ-05, DQ-07, DQ-14, DQ-17, DQ-04 of docs/audit/data_qc_audit.md)

1. **Label tables are now scripted (DQ-02).** `scripts/01b_build_label_tables.py` rebuilds all ten `data/interim/labels/*.csv` files and `data/raw/okada/snrna-seq-data/per_sample_summary.csv` from the supplementary files under `data/raw/<study>/supp/` and the deposits; 10 of 11 tables are byte-identical to the 2026-10-17 files (`results/qc/label_tables_verification.csv`). The one difference: `liu_rawfile_to_sample.csv` columns `unmatched` / `matched_by_normalised_cellid` are now computed with the final ingest regex (`dmg_atlas.ingest.liu_harmonise_id`; 30 cells differ, sum of matches = 8,984 = cells recovered at ingest); `file` and `metadata_sample` — the columns the manifest uses — are unchanged. The DeSisto table is rebuilt from the ScPCA API project record `data/raw/desisto/supp/SCPCP000001_api_project.json` (fetched 2026-10-19, sha256 in `MANIFEST_tumour.csv`), which reproduces the `extra` column exactly. Note on `liu_geo_metadata_sample_summary.csv`: `n_malig` counts author annotations containing "like" (incl. `frozen-OPC-like-b`); cycling malignant cells (`G2M`, `S`) are not counted — the column is informational only.
2. **Non-GEO downloads are now scripted (DQ-02).** `scripts/01c_fetch_nongeo.py` lists the Collot (Zenodo 17922773 + Europe PMC supplement), Okada (UCSC Cell Browser) and ScPCA (API records + dataset zip) fetches with URL, size, sha256 and the Zenodo md5s, and re-hashed all 21 local files on 2026-10-19: 21/21 match (`results/qc/nongeo_fetch_verification.csv`). The ScPCA zip is served from a per-download pre-signed S3 URL and needs the token procedure described in the script docstring.
3. **Manifest regenerated from the script (DQ-05, DQ-07, DQ-14).** `02_build_sample_manifest.py` now encodes the G34R/V → `k27m_status = 'WT'` rule that had been applied by hand, sets `species = 'human'` for every row (was blank in 209 rows) and derives `ROOT` from its own path (as do `01_fetch_tumour_geo.py`, `02_qc_spatial_sections.py`, `03_subset_braun_hindbrain.py`, `dl_ranged.py`). `data/processed/sample_manifest.csv` was regenerated (previous copy `sample_manifest.pre_audit.csv`); the only value changes are `species` and 16 `k27m_status` cells that the earlier pandas re-save had turned from `n/a` into blanks.
4. **`n_cells_deposited` semantics (DQ-17).** The column mixes author-QC cells (Jessa, Liu), matrix barcodes (DeSisto, Zang, Collot, Okada, Filbin, LaBelle) and the portal estimate (ScPCA); the headline "334,439 deposited cells/nuclei" is therefore a mixed count. Per-sample barcode counts before QC are in `results/qc/qc_per_sample.csv` (`cells_before_qc`). Separate columns were not added in this remediation.
5. **Liu deposit (DQ-04).** The raw files come in three annotations (23,686 / 32,617 / 55,765 genes); the 16 files with 23,686 genes carry no MT- genes. See docs/phase2_qc_notes.md, Audit corrections 3–4.
6. **Reference gene sets (MC-03).** `scripts/01d_reference_gene_sets.py` fetched the Liu 2022 supplementary workbook (`data/raw/liu/supp/41588_2022_1236_MOESM3_ESM.xlsx`, sha256 in the manifest) and showed that the `Liu2022_*` sets in `results/tables/genesets/phase6_gene_sets.csv` were in-house Wilcoxon DE sets on the atlas's own Liu data (author state labels), not the published Table S2a (Jaccard 0.27–0.55 against S2a; 0.89–1.00 against the re-derived DE recipe). The Filbin 2018 Science tables could not be downloaded by script (Cloudflare challenge at science.org; proof-of-work page at PMC) — gap recorded in `results/tables/genesets/reference_gene_sets_provenance.csv`.
